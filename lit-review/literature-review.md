The essential properties of the [Zoe](https://docs.agoric.com/guides/zoe/) escrow service are the same as the 42 line escrow contract in the [2013 Dr. SES paper](2013-distributed-electronic-rights.md). The mint / purse pattern and amount math properties are essentially unchanged since the [2000 paper on capability-based financial instruments](2000-capability-financial-instruments.md). 2015 marked the first steps toward [formal reasoning about these patterns](2015-reasoning-risk-trust.md). In 2020, a [Chainmail](2020-holistic-specifications.md) formalism was developed, and in [2022](2022-necessity-specifications.md) the techniques were applied more broadly and formalized in coq. In [2026](2026-robust-safety-back-translation.md), Mück et al. extend the [2017 OCPL](2017-robust-compositional-verification.md) style of robust-safety reasoning from safe high-level languages down to unsafe, C-like ones, recovering robust safety via compilation to a capability machine.
